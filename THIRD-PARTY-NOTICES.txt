Jarvis free neural voice client

This application uses edge-tts 7.3.1, copyright Christopher Down and rany.
Upstream source: https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts/tree/7.3.1
Source release: https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts/archive/refs/tags/7.3.1.tar.gz
The upstream client is unmodified. Most files are LGPLv3; its srt_composer.py is
MIT-licensed. Full upstream notices are in EDGE-TTS-LICENSE.txt, with GPLv3 in
GPL-3.0.txt. Microsoft voice models are not distributed by this application.

The reviewable Jarvis source, requirements.txt and BUILD-EXE.bat are provided
alongside the Windows build. To use a modified, interface-compatible edge-tts,
run Jarvis from source, install the modified package into its .venv, and run
app.py; or rebuild using BUILD-EXE.bat. No code-signature check in Jarvis prevents
such a rebuild. Modification/rebuilding and reverse engineering for debugging
modifications to this library are permitted for that purpose.

The LGPL applies to the edge-tts library; it does not grant ownership of or
unlimited access to Microsoft's online speech service. Service terms,
availability and voice availability are controlled by the service provider.
